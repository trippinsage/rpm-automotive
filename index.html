Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit. Can’t find an answer? Give us a call.

Do I need an appointment for an oil change or inspection?

Walk-ins are welcome for oil changes and inspections. Calling ahead at (709) 279-5282 guarantees a spot and a shorter wait. Most services are completed same day.

What brands of tires do you carry?

We’re an authorized Sailun and Haida tire dealer with full inventory of all season and all terrain tires for cars, SUVs, and trucks. Every set includes professional mounting, balancing, and alignment.

How much does undercoating cost?

Undercoating prices can vary depending on the vehicle and condition. Call (709) 279-5282 for a quote. Every job includes full inspection, surface prep, premium rubberized coating, and final quality check.

What areas does RPM Automotive serve?

We operate from two shop locations on the Burin Peninsula, NL. Families across the province keep coming back for our professional service, tires, undercoating, and full towing fleet.

Do you do engine and transmission work?

Yes. Beyond our core services we handle full engine diagnostics, engine repairs, and transmission work. Bring it in and we’ll diagnose it honestly.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, debit, Visa, and Mastercard. For larger jobs we also offer flexible payment options.
